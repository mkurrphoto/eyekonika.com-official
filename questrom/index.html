01

Settle in, and stay settled

Deep chairs, wide tables, and light that lasts all afternoon. No music engineered to move you along, no table-turning, no one hovering with the check. Take a three-hour stretch and have nothing at all asked of you.

Every seat has power in reach and a signal that holds through a video call, so the room works whether you brought a laptop, a legal pad, or a co-founder.

Nobody here will ever ask how long you’ve been sitting there.

02

Then look up

The best thing in the room isn’t the coffee — it’s who else came in. Founders on draft twelve, a doctoral student two chapters from done, someone sketching a menu for a restaurant that doesn’t exist yet.

“What are you working on?” is a normal question here, not an intrusion. Whoever’s on bar has heard both sides of most problems and will happily point two tables at each other. Some afternoons you work straight through. Some afternoons fifteen minutes with a stranger changes what you do next.

The person two tables over is building something too.

Everything already handled

The small stuff, sorted, so the big stuff gets your attention.

No hunting for a socket. No asking for the password. No fifteen-minute timer on the wi-fi. The room is set up the way you would set it up.

Power

An outlet at every single seat.

Not one shared strip by the window. Thirty-one seats, thirty-one outlets, plus USB‑C at the long table and the banquette. Bring the laptop that stopped holding a charge two years ago.

Room to meet

A long table for eight, and a back corner for two.

Who you’ll meet

A room is only as good as the people who keep coming back to it.

— painted above the pass, since the week we opened

  • Regulars, not traffic. The same forty or so faces most weeks, which is why introductions actually stick.
  • Staff who connect people. Ask what the long table is working on today. You’ll get a real answer.
  • Thursday evenings. An open hour from five: bring the messy version of an idea, leave with three opinions on it.
  • A board by the door. Cards for what people are looking for — a designer, a test reader, a second pair of hands.

About the price

We run roughly 15% above the chain on the corner, and we’d rather explain that than hide it.

That difference buys the room: the outlets, the fibre, the seat nobody asks you to give up, and beans bought at a price the farm agreed to.

Certified organic, and traceable. Every lot we pour is USDA-certified and bought on a published farm-gate price. The current origin is chalked on the board behind the espresso machine — ask, and someone will tell you the farm’s name.

Walk out with more momentum than you walked in with.
