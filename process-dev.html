Our Process

Every step,
in the open.

From your first idea to delivery — documented, approved, and refined. No surprises, no guesswork.

01

Discovery & Consultation

We learn your vision.

We discuss your idea in full — every option, every possibility, what’s achievable and what it costs. We walk you through the details and draw up the contracts. No surprises, no guesswork.

02

Design & Revision

Your image, brought to life.

Our team builds the 3D design from the ground up — visiting your location if needed to capture buildings, monuments, or any subject with precision. You review every draft and we revise until it’s exactly as you imagined.

03

Prototype & Final Approval

You see it before we make it.

Once the design is finalized, we produce a prototype — detailed images sent directly to you, or a physical piece in your hands if needed. Nothing moves to production until you’re certain every detail is exactly right.

04

Production

Precision cut into crystal.

High-precision femtosecond lasers etch your design deep into premium optical K9 crystal. One piece or one thousand — every unit held to the same uncompromising standard.

Shipping & Implementation

Delivered. We stay with you.

We make shipping easy — delivering directly to you or coordinating qualified carriers. When your engravings arrive, we stay at your side: helping plan your campaign, advising on strategy, and answering every question. Reorder pricing is among the most competitive you’ll find.
