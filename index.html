Eyekonika

We make iconic and eye-catching 3D glass engravings for elite memorabilia and profoundly artistic marketing. With intricate designs etched deep into optical glass using femto-second lasers, these small-yet-monumental engravings stand out as a weapon of choice for elite marketing, instutional memorabilia, corporate gifts, and high-value fundraising.

  • Learn more

Step #1: Imagining and Imaging

We walk you through the options, the applications, the uses, and the undless possibilities that our services provide and then draft a plan for your project including a production quote. Then the excitement begins; we deploy a team of aerial imaging specialists to your location and take the necessary photos, vidoeos, scans and measurements.

  • Learn more

Step #2: Design and Production

Our team of professional designers and engineers takes your idea and makes it a reality, implanting your thoughts deep into optical glass. Our specialists guide the product throught the manufacturing process following the specifications we agree on with you. You are supplied with prototype images, updates on design and production, and you can make changes up until the moment the laser hits the glass. This results in a design and finished product of stunning precision and meticulous adherance to the set guidelines.

Step #3: Delivery and Implementation

After the product arives Eyekonika doesn't leave you! We can help design and implement your programs using our product, market the product, and generally stand behind our work and provide guidance or consultation where possible.
