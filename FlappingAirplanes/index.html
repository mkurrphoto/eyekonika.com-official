The Great Minds Card
The brief

Work alone. Don't think alone.

The Great Minds Card is a free loyalty card for the solo builders who already work out of our café — every drink earns a stamp, and every card is a front-of-the-line seat at Big Idea Night, our Thursday evening of short talks and real introductions.

Winning a member costs us a card and a free eighth coffee; keeping one pays for years — the weekday focus visits, the second cup and the pastry, the evening seats that would otherwise sit empty, and the friend they bring next Thursday. And Big Idea Night is what makes it worthwhile for our members: they leave with focused hours and one useful connection, not just a caffeine buzz.

Coffee, conversation, big ideas.
