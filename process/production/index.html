Step 04 — Production

Made to an exacting standard, at any scale.

Your approved concept enters controlled production: calibrated subsurface engraving in premium K9 optical glass, with quality verification at every stage from prep through finishing.

Production scrubber

Material Prep

K9 optical glass is selected, cleaned, and measured for optical clarity.

Quality checkpoints

  • Laser power and focus window validation before each run.
  • Depth fidelity checks against approved proof references.
  • Clarity and edge artifact inspection before packaging.
  • Consistency controls for single pieces and high-volume batches.

Every unit follows the same quality rubric regardless of quantity.
