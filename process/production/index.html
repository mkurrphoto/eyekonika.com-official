Step 04 — Production

Made to an exacting standard, at any scale.

Your approved concept enters controlled production: calibrated subsurface engraving in premium K9 optical glass, with quality verification at every stage from material selection through to finished packaging. The piece that leaves is exactly the piece that was approved.

Five stages

Production from first material to final dispatch

Material Prep

K9 optical glass blanks are selected and inspected for internal clarity. Each piece is cleaned and measured to specification before a single laser path is configured.

Quality pillars

What we verify at every point of production

⬡

Laser Validation

Power, frequency, and focus window are verified against a calibration baseline before each production run begins. Parameters are logged and held on file.

◎

Depth Fidelity

Every engraved piece is checked against the approved depth map to confirm that three-dimensional geometry matches the signed-off proof precisely.

✦

Edge & Clarity

Technicians inspect each piece for edge sharpness, internal clarity, and the absence of artefacts before it is cleared for finishing and packaging.

⊞

Batch Consistency

For multi-unit commissions, consistency controls ensure that the first piece and the last piece are indistinguishable — regardless of production volume.
