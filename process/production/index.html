Step 04 — Production

High-precision lasers. Premium optical K9 crystal.

Your approved design enters mass production. Femtosecond lasers etch thousands of microscopic dots deep inside the optical glass — the beams pass through the surface without marking it, engraving the image entirely beneath. Depending on the machine, up to 200,000 dots are placed every minute. The result is a design that is permanent, precise, and visible only when light finds it.

The method

Industry standard and beyond

Material Prep

Optical-grade K9 crystal blanks are selected and inspected for internal clarity. Each piece is cleaned and measured to specification before a single laser path is configured. K9 crystal is chosen for its exceptional light transmission — it is what makes the engraving glow.
