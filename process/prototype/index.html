Step 03 — Prototype

See it from every angle before it exists.

Prototype is the moment the concept becomes something you can evaluate with confidence. We build a precise digital representation of your piece — one that shows how depth, light, and geometry will interact inside real optical glass — and we refine it until your approval is decisive, not tentative.

What you receive

Three deliverables before a single piece is made

⬡

Digital Proof

A rendered representation of the engraved design as it will appear inside the glass — including light-reactive geometry, perspective, and depth layering. Not an approximation. A precise simulation of the physical result.

◈

Depth Map Review

We walk through the depth map — the three-dimensional point-cloud that tells the laser where to engrave — so you can understand exactly how detail hierarchy, shadow, and foreground-to-background relationships have been interpreted.

✦

Revision Rounds

Your feedback is incorporated into subsequent proofs until the result is precisely what the Vision and Discovery sessions described. We do not move to production until you have signed off — in writing — on a final proof you are fully satisfied with.

"Confidence at the approval stage is not a formality. It is the last moment to shape the piece. We treat it that way."

How approval works

Once you have reviewed the digital proof and depth map, and any revisions have been incorporated, we issue a formal sign-off document. Your signature on that document is the gate that opens production. Nothing is manufactured before it. Once production begins on approved work, the specification is fixed — which is why Prototype exists: to make the point of commitment one you arrive at with complete clarity, not one you cross by default.
