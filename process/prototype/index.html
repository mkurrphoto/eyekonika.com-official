Step 03 — Prototype & Final Approval

Nothing enters production until you are certain it is right.

Once the design is finalized, we produce a prototype. You receive detailed images showing exactly how the engraving will appear in the finished crystal. If you want to hold the actual piece before approving the full production run, we can send a physical prototype directly to you.

What you receive

Three checkpoints before full production begins

⬡

Prototype Images

We photograph the prototype from multiple angles in controlled lighting so you can evaluate depth, detail fidelity, and visual weight. So many clients are surprised that even the smallest details — doors, windows, wall plaques — are accurately included.

◈

Physical Prototype Option

If you want to hold the piece, you can. A physical prototype shipped directly to you is available for clients who want tactile confirmation before signing off on a full production order. You see it, you feel it, you decide.

✦

Final Approval

Your explicit approval is required before production begins — we do not make assumptions. Once you are certain the design is just right, you sign off and production starts without delay.

“They even have the windows!! So many customers who carefully look at our engravings are surprised that even small details are accurately included — making the sense of quality and exclusivity stronger.”

How approval leads into production

Once you have reviewed the prototype — images or physical piece — and any final adjustments are made, you give the go-ahead. That is the gate that opens production. Nothing is manufactured before it. Once production begins on approved work, the specification is fixed, which is exactly why this step exists: to ensure you arrive at that commitment with complete confidence, not hesitation.
