Step 01 — Discovery & Consultation

We discuss your idea with you, openly and in full.

This is not a form to fill out or a catalog to browse. It is a real conversation — where we listen to what you have in mind, walk you through every available option, explain exactly what is and isn’t possible, and make sure you understand everything before anything is committed to paper.

What we cover together

Three things we establish before a single dollar is quoted

◇

Your Iconic Image

Every organization has an image associated with it. For some it is a logo, for others a building, a landmark, a person, or a concept. We work with you to identify the specific image that captures what people remember about you — and ensure it will translate beautifully into three-dimensional glass.

◈

Your Options

We walk you through every variable: size, crystal grade, quantity tiers, base options, gift box style, and display solutions. Nothing is hidden. By the end of this session you know exactly what each choice involves and what it costs.

✦

The Contracts

Once you know what you want, we draw up the contracts clearly and completely. No surprises during production, no unexpected charges at delivery. What is agreed is what is built.

“You come to us with your idea, your dream — and we work with you to make it a reality. We handle everything from design to sourcing to delivery, so you can focus on what matters.”

What happens after consultation

At the close of this session, we have a shared understanding of your project — your image, your quantities, your timeline, and your budget range. The next step, Design & Revision, is where our team begins the actual 3D modeling work. Visiting your location if needed, building from your existing assets, and producing a design you can review and refine until it is exactly right.
