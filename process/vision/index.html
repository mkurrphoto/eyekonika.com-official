Step 01 — Vision

It starts with a feeling.

Your first session is not a briefing form. It is a conversation — open, unhurried — where we listen for the feeling a piece must carry. Prestige. Legacy. Belonging. Gratitude. We translate that feeling into a creative direction before a single measurement is taken.

What we explore together

Three lines of inquiry that shape every piece

◇

Your Story

Every commission has an origin. We want to understand it — the milestone being marked, the relationship being honoured, the institution being represented. The story becomes the invisible architecture of the piece.

◈

Your Audience

Who will encounter this piece? A board room, a gala stage, a collector's shelf, a personal study? The audience shapes the visual language — what reads at distance, what rewards closer attention, what communicates before a word is spoken.

✦

Your Legacy

Glass endures. We ask what this piece should still communicate in twenty years — what the person receiving it should feel every time they see it. The answer shapes quality decisions that cannot be revisited after production begins.

"The most important creative decisions happen before the first sketch. What we are really designing is the feeling left behind after someone looks away."

What happens after Vision

At the close of Vision, we have a shared sense of direction — not a spec sheet, not a budget, not a timeline. Those come later, and they come correctly, because they are built on a foundation we have both contributed to. The next step, Discovery, is where that direction becomes a precise set of requirements. Every variable that affects production and pricing is mapped before a quote is ever written.
