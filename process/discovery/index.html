Step 02 — Design & Revision

Your image, built from the ground up.

Our team begins constructing the 3D model of your chosen subject. For buildings, monuments, or physical structures we may visit your location directly — using professional equipment including drone photography to capture every detail accurately. For logos and abstract concepts, we work from your existing assets and creative direction.

What this step involves

How we build your design

⬡

3D Modeling

We build a precise three-dimensional model of your subject. Whether it is a campus building, a church facade, a corporate logo, or a custom concept, every element is modeled with the level of detail that subsurface laser engraving demands.

◎

Location Visits

For physical subjects — buildings, landmarks, monuments — our team visits your site if needed. We hold FAA Part 107 drone pilot certification, allowing us to capture aerial reference that no ground-level photograph could provide.

⊞

Review & Revision

You review every draft. If something is not right — an angle, a detail, a proportion — we revise it. We go back and forth until the design is exactly as you imagined. Nothing moves to prototype until you say it does.

◷

No Rush

We are attentive to each individual project. You will not be ordering a design off a rack. Every design is developed from your idea specifically, and we take the time to get it right before moving forward.

Why the design stage matters so much

Nothing in the glass can be adjusted after production. The 3D design is the foundation everything else is built on — and it is the last moment where a change costs nothing. Getting it right here, through careful revision rounds, means the prototype and production stages proceed smoothly, quickly, and exactly to specification. This is where Eyekonika’s attention to each individual project makes the biggest difference.
